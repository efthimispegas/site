The proposed capabilities cover product strategy, UX/UI design, web and mobile engineering, AI and automation, and cloud platforms.

How does a project start?

Start with a conversation about the problem, your users and the outcome you want. Discovery can then define priorities and the first delivery milestone.

How is the timeline agreed?

Scope, integrations and dependencies determine the delivery plan. Milestones and review points are agreed before work begins.

How do we stay involved?

The proposed collaboration model uses shared priorities, visible progress and regular reviews so decisions stay close to the work.

Who owns the source code?

Ownership, source access and licensing are agreed in the project contract.

What happens after launch?

Support, availability and ongoing development are scoped and agreed separately.
