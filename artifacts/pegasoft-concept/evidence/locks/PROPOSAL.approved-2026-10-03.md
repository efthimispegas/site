## Art direction: precision in motion

The site's story is one idea carried from strategy through design, engineering, deployment and scale. The silver container embodies that idea. The lift conveys deliberate construction, the truck dependable delivery, the ship coordinated scale, and the jet precision and ambition. These remain the reference's transport subjects as requested; the copy identifies Pegasoft as a technology company.

Black lacquer, carbon, brushed titanium and restrained blue illumination. Strong negative space; large upright typography; fine technical labels; rounded CTAs; editorial grids rather than decorative dashboard UI. The reference's composition, subject positions and entrance order remain the target. No italic typography.

## Typography

An original Pegasoft typographic system using existing licensed faces; this does not imply a newly designed font file.

- **Manrope, upright 600–700:** display headlines and large numerals. Compact tracking around −0.045em, deliberate 2–3-line compositions, strong full-size punctuation. Hero trial copy: “EVERY IDEA. / ENGINEERED / TO MATTER.”
- **DM Sans, upright 400–500:** prose, navigation, tabs and forms. Comfortable leading and quieter sentence-case contact headlines; this links to NexDash's softer typography direction.
- **IBM Plex Mono, upright 400:** tiny chapter numbers, loader/state labels and technical metadata only.

Live DOM inspection found NexDash's contact heading uses DM Sans 700 and its body uses Inter; United Carriers uses BT Steinhart for its headings. Pegasoft uses its own pairing and spacing. ## Motion reconstruction method

Increasing uniform watch sampling is insufficient: watch caps its uniform sampler at 2 fps, while these source recordings contain 60 frames per second. Do not interpolate frames, change the skill's global configuration or treat a denser contact sheet as a timing specification. Use a two-stage local process instead:

1. **Screen native frames numerically.** Decode every source frame of loader, hero and main at a small analysis resolution, retaining actual source PTS. Compute change energy in separate scene, central-action and headline regions; exclude the reference's sticky award strip. This inexpensive pass locates candidate fast events without sending thousands of images into the model context. It does not identify semantic objects or establish trajectories.
2. **Inspect event windows, not a uniform frame grid.** Preserve source-cadence clips around loader pulse/aperture, hero entry/exit, lift attachment, camera pivot/truck handoff, road pivot, road-to-deck alignment, ship pullback/clouds and jet/trust masking. Use normal and slow playback, timestamped frame steps and watch cue frames at detected extrema. Reuse the existing narration transcript. Expand only a disputed window or object crop; do not retranscribe or resample all five videos unnecessarily.
3. **Separate motion drivers.** Loader rings, pulse, aperture and headline staggering use elapsed time; globe rotation also has a continuous clock. Transport/camera/reveals use physical scroll position. Cursor/hover effects use pointer input plus elapsed response time. A region-change curve locates an event, but is never an easing curve for these different drivers.
4. **Measure live rendered geometry.** Use ordinary forward/reverse wheel input and read-only DOM rectangles, transforms, opacity and mask properties. Initial geometry samples are saved in [live-geometry.json](evidence/motion/live-geometry.json). They are coarse checkpoints at the recorded viewport, not RAF traces, exact pin-boundary measurements or a complete fitted timeline. Next calibration uses smaller steps around each event, actual scrollY after settling, forward/reverse pairs at matching positions, and frame-level rendered response traces where time lag matters. Do not use internal reference timeline state as evidence.
5. **Build motion contracts before section implementation.** The [motion contract registry](evidence/motion/motion-contracts.json) records event drivers, source windows and continuity constraints; unmeasured fields remain null. For each event add measured start/end, normalized x/y/width/height, camera/view change, opacity, occlusion order, attachment constraints, pin interval, easing hypothesis and uncertainty. Follow one silver container through every handoff. Couple jet, shadow and trust mask to actual wing/fuselage/tail landmarks. Fit object geometry and progress before secondary atmosphere; distinguish measured values from design decisions necessitated by the new jet.
6. **Validate the rendered slice.** Compare normal playback and aligned frames/overlays at identical viewport, actual elapsed time for the loader and actual scroll landmarks for transport. Inspect velocity/scale continuity, entry overlaps, attachment/release order, clipping and reverse-scroll restoration. Review both source cadence and normal user interaction; a perfect still or passing numeric check cannot approve the motion. Resolve residual errors before the user's final section review.

Evidence roles remain explicit: old contact sheets = visually inspected structural evidence; native-frame CSVs = automated numeric screening; clips = source-cadence review proxies; additional watch cues = locally inspected event images; live DOM samples = rendered geometry checkpoints. Exact timing, trajectories, pin boundaries and easing remain to be qualified through calibration and section review. The [motion viewer](review/motion.html) exposes requested versus actually presented proxy timestamps so browser seeking is not mislabeled as exact source-frame correspondence.

Current coverage: 25,295 native frames screened (174 loader, 855 hero, 24,266 main), 10 source-cadence clips, 27 additional cue images visually inspected, and 10 live forward/reverse geometry checkpoints. The [findings record](evidence/motion/findings.json) distinguishes observations from measurement limits. In particular, the loader's expanding outer disk and small central disk need separate timing; late jet-window change maxima include testimonial scrolling, so numerical peaks alone cannot define the crossing. Source PTS spacing is approximately 16.667 ms; faster motion than the recording can resolve remains unavailable evidence.
